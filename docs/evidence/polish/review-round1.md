# Round 1 visual review (left and right walls)

## Scores (AFTER)

**(a) Effortful: 7/10**
- Dense, purposeful dressing: grow shelf with magenta glow, lockers with name tapes, whiteboard with sticky notes, mission patches, vents, cable trays.
- Floor is a huge empty dark disc and the lower third of every frame is dead space. The room is detailed only at wall height.
- Ceiling strips are flat cream bands with hard edges and two stubby vertical "drop" bars that read as geometry errors, not light fittings.

**(b) Consistent style: 6/10**
- Wall panels, trims and the stencilled equipment faces (WATER RECYCLER, O2 SCRUBBER B, AIRLOCK B) share a palette and hazard-stripe language.
- Fonts clash: rounded-bold screen type, condensed stencil, and a handwriting-ish whiteboard face. The grey-green machines look flat and cartoon-shaded beside the textured panels.
- The red barrel and the cyan/teal lockers are much more saturated than the rest and look dropped in. Blue cylinders left of the water recycler are unexplained.

**(c) Readable text: 5/10**
- Big titles read (WATER RECYCLER, O2 SCRUBBER B, POWER 100). Small text will not at 1200 px per eye: "PRESSURE DOOR · CYCLE BEFORE O...", locker "CREW 1/2", whiteboard lines, patch lettering, the HUD "Two-finger hold 2 s".
- Several labels are clipped at both ends: "RES A · DRY GC", "ROPONICS · B", "L 100 PLAN", "...ns your viewer QR". The text is wider than its sign.
- The whiteboard's strikethrough lines and tiny stickies are noise at headset size.

**(d) Believable Mars base: 6/10**
- Good: riveted panels, scuffed lower band, vents, blue/amber conduit runs, a hydroponics rack that is plausible.
- Toy cues: dials and LEDs are flat decals, the airlock is a slab with a cartoon red wheel, and the lockers and machines float with no contact shadow.
- The lighting is flat. Warm strips cast no visible pool on walls or floor, and the cool-versus-warm "shelter" contrast from ART.md is hardly visible.

## Five fixes, in priority order
1. Fit every sign to its text: size the plate from the measured string width, add padding, shorten the strings (e.g. "HYDROPONICS", "STORES A"), and never clip.
2. Raise the minimum text height on small labels (pressure-door sub-line, locker "CREW n", patch text) to the card size, or delete the line; drop the whiteboard strikethrough.
3. Rebuild the ceiling strips as slim, rounded, dimmer emissive tubes with a soft glow sprite and a warm gradient pool on the wall below, and remove the two vertical stub bars.
4. Add grounding: blob contact shadows or AO decals under the machines, lockers, shelves and barrel, plus a warm light pool and scuff or marking decals on the floor.
5. Pull the saturation of the barrel and the lockers toward the painted-metal palette, put one typeface and one stencil style on all equipment faces, and give the dials depth (bezel plus glass highlight).

## Do NOT change
The riveted panel texture with the darker lower wear band, the hazard stripes, the grow-light shelf glow and the blue/amber conduit trims: together they already carry the lived-in look.
