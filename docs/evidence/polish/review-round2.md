# Round 2 visual review (side walls and wall screens)

## Room scores

**(a) Effortful: 8/10**
- Dense, layered dressing: grow shelf with magenta glow, crates, lockers with name plates, whiteboard with stickies, patches, vents, floor chevrons, hazard-striped machines.
- Ceiling is now slim glowing tubes with a warm pool. It is much better, but it is one repeated curve, and the floor is still a big empty dark disc.
- Machines, lockers and shelves still look planted, with weak contact shadows.

**(b) Consistent style: 7/10**
- Signs now share a stencil family (WATER RECYCLER, O2 SCRUBBER B, AIRLOCK B). Locker and barrel colours are muted into the palette.
- The whiteboard handwriting and the rounded screen type are still a second and third voice. Wall screens are dark, glossy and modern against the matte, cartoon-shaded machines.
- The two lockers are flat colour slabs with no panel wear, so they look less textured than the walls.

**(c) Readable text: 6/10**
- Titles read well (WATER RECYCLER, SOL 100 PLAN, POWER 100). Signs now fit; no clipping on STORES A or HYDROPONICS.
- Locker names are readable but small. The HUD line ("Two-finger hold 2 s (out of the vie...)") and the big POWER card are cut off at the frame edge.
- Tiny text remains: whiteboard checklist, "CREW" patch lettering, the DEBUG overlay.

**(d) Believable Mars base: 7/10**
- Riveted panels, conduit runs, vents, warm strips and the magenta grow light sell shelter.
- Dials and LEDs now have bezels but are still decals. The airlock is a slab with a cartoon wheel.
- The floor and the unlit left screen read as empty, not as a base.

## Screen scores

**(e) Designed: 8/10**
- POWER has a ring gauge, a sparkline, icon chips and an ON pill. MONEY has KPI tiles. WAITING has a status header, an Earth-delay card and a progress bar.
- WAITING is the strongest. MONEY and POWER are close behind.

**(f) Legible hierarchy: 6/10**
- The hero numbers ($180.0M, 298 days, 100%) dominate correctly.
- Secondary labels ("Saved so far", "Committed", "Solar model: Opportunity rover") are about 1/6 the size of the hero text and will mush at headset resolution.
- Robot nametags, speech bubbles and ICE DRILL cards overlap the screens (MONEY header, POWER "kWh per sol"). The screens have no safe zone.

**(g) Consistent with the room: 7/10**
- Agent colour accents and the dark navy panels match the robots and HUD.
- Screens are cleaner and bluer than the warm, scuffed room. They have no bezel wear or screen glow spilling onto the wall.
- The left wall's dark screen is blank apart from a clipped QR hint.

## Round 1 fixes

1. Fit signs to text: **landed**.
2. Raise minimum text height: **partly landed**. Locker plates and signs are bigger, but screen sub-text and whiteboard text are still tiny. The strikethrough is gone on the left wall only.
3. Ceiling strips rebuilt: **landed**. They are slim tubes, the stubs are gone and there is a warm pool. From inside they are still bright, flat cream bands in some views.
4. Grounding: **partly landed**. Floor chevrons and a trim ring are present, but there are no visible contact shadows under machines or lockers.
5. Palette, one stencil style, dial depth: **landed**.

## Next five fixes

1. Add soft contact-shadow decals and a warm floor light pool under every machine, locker, shelf, barrel and table, and fill the empty central floor with scuffs, a drain, cable runs or a rug.
2. Raise the secondary text on all three screens to at least about 60% of the hero size, and cut the small footer lines ("Solar model...", "budget are SIMULATED") to a single legible line.
3. Keep robot tags, bubbles and cards out of the screen rectangles by raising or offsetting them, so no screen header or gauge is covered.
4. Make the blank left-wall screen and the HUD line show real content or turn off, and keep all card text inside the frame at the default view angle.
5. Add wall-side lighting: a faint cool glow from each screen on the nearby wall, a warm gradient under the ceiling tubes, and a bezel with scuffs on the screens so they sit in the room.

## Leave alone

The riveted panel wall with its wear band, the hazard stripes, the grow-light shelf, the conduit trims, the redesigned WAITING card, and the new fitted stencil signs.
