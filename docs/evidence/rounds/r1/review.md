# Judge review, round 1 (cold viewer)

## 1. Who speaks, what each robot does
Speaker was always clear: coloured speech bubble, matching name pill over the robot, and a bottom subtitle with icon and colour. Robots (from labels and lines):
- Engineer (blue, wrench): keeps the base powered and running; warns about the storm.
- Science (green, microscope): grinds rock samples, wants to show me.
- Supply (orange, crate): buys things, checks deliveries ("two crates missing, I won't pay").
- Life (white cube, drop): air and water.
- Rules (purple cylinder, shield): guards the books, blocks scams, asks Earth to verify. I read it as the "police/accountant".
Weak spot: Science and Life never appear on screen in the inside frames (27, 33, 66, 89), yet Life talks (line 20 in 89) and the card says "SCIENCE WILL SAY". A bubble from a robot I cannot see is confusing.

## 2. Readability (hold = 2.5 s + 0.4 s per word)
Lines are 6-12 words, so 5-7 s hold, spaced ~6.7 s apart. Everything was readable. Risks: line 15 (66-decision) bubble is clipped off the top of the frame and "WHAT DO WE GIVE UP?" is hidden behind Rules' head; line 18 (78) is still on screen while the card already says "COMMANDER CHOSE", so the subtitle and result compete. In 99 the end-card text sits on top of Rules' bubble, unreadable.

## 3. Robotic / rushed / confusing / unmotivated
- Dialogue is uniformly "report in complete sentences" (lines 8-14); no hesitation, humour or disagreement, so it reads as a script.
- Line 1 "Welcome, Commander. The crew has the base handled" followed by line 3 "Two crates are missing" (18.8 s): a problem is stated, then dropped. Why do I care about crates? Never resolved.
- Line 5 "head inside the base right now" (24-entering) gives no reason I can feel; the storm is only a sky tint.
- Line 7 "Quiet day... nothing suspicious" (27-inside) comes right before a crisis, fine as setup, but the screens (Use 517, Cash $9.0M, "Saved so far $2k") are unexplained jargon.
- Line 10-14: the scam sequence is the best part but numbers (triple price, new bank account) are only spoken, not shown on a card; I had to trust Rules.
- 78-consequence: "25 MINUTES LATER" card covers the ICE DRILL OFFLINE sign and Rules; Life's line about nine days of water appears with no number on screen.
- 89-later: "$1." and "The ne..." on the right panel are cut off; the payoff number is unreadable.
- Life/Rules ("Air is fresh"): line 6 is pure filler.

## 4. Did I know what to do?
Title card to outside: fine. Entering the base: Engineer tells me, but nothing says how (walk? look? the debug camera hides this). Decision: "LOOK AT the card" hint plus gaze highlight (66) is clear. After the decision I am passive for ~40 s (132-170 s). The explore phase ("Rules, tap to ask", "LOOK AT Engineer") offers two different verbs (tap vs look) and no stated purpose. 106-explore-reality looks identical to 104; I did not see "where we really are" change anything.

## 5. One idea
"An AI crew runs a Mars base's purchasing on its own, a rules robot catches a scam supplier, and only hands the human the big call: what to switch off while Earth verifies." The closing card "An AI crew that keeps a Mars base alive. And knows when to ask you." says it, but it appears at 99 over clutter and fades fast.

## Scores
1. Speaker/roles clarity: 8
2. Readability: 8
3. Not robotic/rushed/confusing: 5
4. Knowing what to do next: 6
5. One idea: 6

4. 78-consequence: "25 MINUTES LATER" card hides the offline sign; Life's "nine days" has no on-screen number; fix: move the card up and put "Water: 9 days" on the Use panel.
5. 106-explore-reality vs 104: no visible change when "where we really are" is offered; fix: cut to a real Jezero image/real-data frame with citation.
6. Lines 8-14: pure statement dialogue; fix: let Engineer push back on Supply and Rules interrupt, shorter and punchier.
7. Line 3 "two crates missing" (03/16 frames) is dropped; fix: resolve it in the epilogue or cut it.
8. 24-entering: no reason to go inside; fix: add a visible dust wall and storm audio/sky change before line 5.
