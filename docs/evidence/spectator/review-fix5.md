# Spectator view review, fix5 (frames 001-099, odd frames viewed)

Ranked issues, most damaging first.

1. **Decision phase is a wall of overlays (061, 063, 065, 067, 069).** The subtitle box moves up to mid-screen and sits on top of the big decision card. The 3D option panels show through both as ghosted, half-transparent duplicate text ("Shut down the drill" appears three times). In 061 the subtitle covers the Rules robot and the option panels. Fix: while the card is up, pin the subtitle above the card, or shrink the card to about 35% of the height and drop the subtitle to one line. Hide the in-world option panels on the mirror.
2. **Cue pill collides with the pill/bubble content (009, 011, 061, 063, 065, 067, 069, 097).** "LOOK AT AN OPTION · TAP TO CHOOSE" is drawn over the Life bubble text in 065 ("Stopping the ice drill pauses our water supply" is unreadable) and over the Science bubble in 069. In 009, 013 and 097 it overlaps the Engineer/Rules labels. Fix: reserve a top band for the pill and push in-world bubbles and labels out of it, or draw the pill beneath them.
3. **Bubbles shown in the world are cut off or edge-on (005, 019, 021, 029, 033, 035, 063, 071, 075, 083).** Speech bubbles are clipped at the screen edge or sit at steep angles: "Arm grinding ancient river-delta rock" in 007 and 009, the Life bubble in 029 and 075, the Rules bubble in 063 and 065. The same words are also in the subtitle. Fix: show the world bubble only when it is on-screen and face-on. Otherwise rely on the subtitle, since duplicated text is just noise.
4. **Name labels are huge, tilted and on top of other things (005, 007, 013, 015, 063).** The labels tilt and distort at the screen edges. "Engineer" blocks the 'LOOK AT' pill in 009. "Science" in 007 (frame 009 of the Engineer scene) hides behind the rock, and "Supply" is nearly invisible. In 005 a blue label fragment fills the top-left corner. Fix: billboard labels to face the camera, cap their size, and fade them when they are off-axis or over 60 degrees to the side.
5. **The story card is not obviously a decision (071, 073).** After the choice, "COMMANDER CHOSE: SHUT DOWN THE DRILL" shows only for one frame (069), and the chosen card is not visually different from the others until 069. The card does leave afterwards, which is good. Fix: hold the chosen state for 3 s with the other two options dimmed more strongly, and add the consequence ("Water: 9 days").
6. **Subtitle box is over scene content, with low contrast on bright backdrops (003, 031, 077, 079).** It covers the lower 15% of the screen and sometimes hides the HUD panels ("Checking with Earth" collides with it in 041 and 053). The text itself is fine. Fix: add a slightly stronger background or move the quote panels above it.
7. **Dim "Supply" label is nearly invisible (025, 031, 057, 063, 081).** It is a translucent orange pill with a ghost look. Viewers can't tell who it is. Fix: use the same fully opaque style as the other labels.
8. **Overlapping 3D panels are hard to read (031, 035, 047).** The Quote Flagged panel is at an extreme angle and clipped ("Total $1.4M" cut off). Fix: keep the camera or panel orientation readable, or lock the camera for quote scenes.
9. **Title card (001) has an odd cursor ring over the "Earth" text, partly hiding the "h".** Fix: hide the reticle during title cards.
10. **Reticle is a small white circle that sometimes sits on text (029, 031, 047).** It obscures "Cash $9.0M" in 031 and 081. Fix: make it smaller or hide it when no tap target is under it.
11. **Closing card text is clipped (091, 093).** "An AI crew that keeps a Mars base alive. And knows when to ask you." is cut off at the left and right edges. It is in 3D, so it is only readable at one head angle. Fix: show the closing line as a flat centred overlay.
12. **Progress badge changes without any indicator (047, 081).** It goes "6 of 7 Commander decides" to "7 of 7 Earth's answer" with no transition. Minor. The top-right badge is also overlapped by tilted labels in 043 and 053 ("Rules" runs through "5 of 7").

## Score: 5/10

The overlays are consistent and large enough, and the decision card clearly states the question and the options. The 3D world is busy. Duplicated text (world bubble plus subtitle plus option panels) and overlapping layers make the decision phase hard to follow, which is the most important moment.
